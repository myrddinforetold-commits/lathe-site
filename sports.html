Rule of thumb

What a good scout does with a spreadsheet.

  • Sort the eligible players by price and take the top ten
  • Assume the youngest players will improve the most
  • Add up a player's ratings and rank on the total

Data-science model

What an analytics team would build.

  • A statistical model made for one question, such as "how good will he be next season?"
  • Strong on numbers: the benchmark to take seriously
  • Cannot read a scout's note, and each new question needs a new model

What we build

One model per job, across the whole club.

A general assistant knows football in general. Each Lathe model is trained on one department's own work, so it knows how your club does it.

01 / Recruitment

Scouting and recruitment

Trained on your scouts' reports, your grading scale and your shortlists.

  • Drafts scouting reports in your format from match data and a scout's notes
  • Turns a written report into ratings on your club's own scale
  • Finds players who fit a described profile across leagues you can't watch every week
  • Flags where a report and the numbers disagree, so someone looks twice
02 / Football

Analysis and coaching

Trained on your analysts' packs and the way your staff talk about the game.

  • Writes opposition reports from event and tracking data
  • Produces post-match summaries for coaches, players and the board, each at the right depth
  • Catalogues set pieces and patterns in your own terminology
  • Answers a coach's question from seasons of your own analysis
03 / Commercial

Partnerships and sales

Trained on your deals, your proposals and your partner results.

  • Builds sponsor proposals from your inventory, audience and past results
  • Writes partner renewal reports from campaign data

  • Surfaces which hospitality and season-ticket accounts are likely to lapse
04 / Supporters

Fans and operations

Trained on your supporter service history and your club's voice.
