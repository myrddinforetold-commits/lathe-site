How it works

Real work in. Proof out. Payment on proof.

01

Provide

Run the Bitcomp client on a machine with a capable GPU. It picks up training jobs from Lathe when the machine is free.

02

Prove

Every job returns its result with a fingerprint of the computation. The network checks the fingerprint and re-runs a sample of jobs.

03

Paid

Work that passes is credited in BCU. Work that fails a check is not paid, and the deposit behind it is at risk.

Proof of compute

Useful work, not wasted work.

Proof of work burns energy on puzzles nobody needs solved. Proof of compute pays for training that a customer is waiting for, and proves it was done properly.

  1. Deposit

    Skin in the game

    A provider posts a deposit before taking work. It is returned in full to honest providers and forfeited by dishonest ones.

  2. Fingerprint

    The computation leaves a trace

    Each result carries a compact signature of the model and hardware that produced it. A swapped model or a faked result does not match.

  3. Spot-check

    A sample is run again

    Trusted machines repeat a random share of jobs and compare. A provider never knows which job will be checked.

  4. Penalty

    Cheating costs more than it earns

    A failed check forfeits the deposit and removes the provider. The arithmetic is set so that honest work is the only profitable strategy.

BCU

One unit. A fixed amount of verified compute.

BCU is how the network keeps account. Providers earn it for verified work. Customers spend it on training and inference. Its price floats, so it reports what compute is actually worth.

Roadmap

Built in the order that earns trust.

  1. Now

    Trusted compute

    Lathe trains on machines it controls while the checks are built and tested.

  2. Next

    Peer network

    Outside providers join. Verified work is tracked as points, off-chain.

  3. Then

    BCU on Base

    Points become BCU. Deposits and payouts move on-chain.

  4. After

    Open platform

    Anyone can bring a training job to the network, not only Lathe.
